# SAFE Metric Metamorphics v1

Status: executable synthetic boundary/metamorphic experiment  
Date: 2026-07-25

## Question

Can an evidence passport distinguish changes in aggregation geometry,
measurement profile, uncertainty support and TOPSIS reference semantics that
may leave a scalar-looking score superficially plausible?

## Implemented checks

- arithmetic, geometric and RMS tensor integration in pure-Python and NumPy;
- arithmetic and geometric factorization identities;
- `geometric ≤ arithmetic ≤ RMS` on positive inputs;
- axis and within-axis order invariance;
- tensor versus aligned-diagonal non-equivalence fixture;
- profile-digest sensitivity to six semantic mutations;
- dynamic-reference TOPSIS rank-reversal fixture;
- fixed-reference TOPSIS rank-invariance control;
- bootstrap lower-confidence-bound gates;
- point-only versus confidence-aware decisions;
- shared-sample versus independence-assumed bootstrap boundary fixture.

The metric vectors and samples are synthetic test fixtures. They are not
reported as empirical results from Kolesnikov, Giudici or the University of
Pavia. The originating SAFE integration family is credited as prior work.

## Run

```bash
./run.sh
```

The two implementations share the same specification and language ecosystem;
their agreement is code-path diversity, not institutional independence.

## Claim ceiling

The suite can establish reproducible counterexamples and implementation
invariants for the frozen fixtures. It cannot establish:

- population prevalence of rank reversal or confidence failures;
- superiority of one SAFE aggregation function;
- legal or regulatory compliance;
- external validity on deployed models;
- correctness of every possible TOPSIS normalization;
- independence of expected labels from the experiment design.

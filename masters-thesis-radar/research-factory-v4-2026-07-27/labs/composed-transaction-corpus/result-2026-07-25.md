## Supported claim

Within the frozen synthetic/hybrid corpus, individually plausible or
signature-valid artifacts were insufficient for a non-compensable delegated
decision. Exact policy identity, evidence freshness/integrity, runtime state,
authority-to-effect composition and measurement support had to be appraised
as distinct predicates. Compensating or point-only ablations created false
allows.

## Claim ceiling

The result does not establish production prevalence, superiority over named
systems, external label independence, legal validity, or deployed
interoperability. The SQL oracle is a second composition implementation, not
an independent cryptographic, statistical or authority-label source. The
measurement fixtures are synthetic; the authority corpus remains partly
synthetic, with a recorded-A2A hybrid lineage.

## Supported claim

Within this frozen synthetic/hybrid corpus, individually plausible or
signature-valid artifacts were insufficient for a non-compensable delegated
decision, including on transactions whose faults cross the
authority×policy, authority×evidence, state×policy and state×evidence
seams that corpus v1 never exercised. Compensating or point-only ablations
created false allows on every family that contains single-fault or
non-artifact-layer denies.

## Claim ceiling

The result does not establish production prevalence, superiority over
named systems, external label independence, legal validity, or deployed
interoperability. Expected labels are author-written; composition-level
agreement is analytic (B1). The state layer is a structural appraisal of
corpus-supplied attestation-result objects, not cryptographic attestation
of any runtime. The authority layer's cryptographic flags are
corpus-supplied. The SQL oracle is a second composition code path, not an
independent cryptographic, statistical or authority-label source. These
disclaimers are embedded in the generated JSON summaries.

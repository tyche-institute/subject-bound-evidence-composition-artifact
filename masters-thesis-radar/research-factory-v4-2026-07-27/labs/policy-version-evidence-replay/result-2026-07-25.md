## Interpretation

In this signed-object test profile, policy presence and policy identity are
not interchangeable. A valid signature on a stale or substituted policy does
not establish that the intended appraisal procedure was active. Treating a
missing policy as permissive adds another false-allow class.

## Claim ceiling

This result is not a Veraison benchmark. It is consistent with and motivated
by the previously recorded Tyche harness/provisioning failures, for which
Veraison itself behaved correctly when the intended policy was supplied.
The experiment does not estimate production prevalence or prove that the
chosen passport fields are sufficient.
